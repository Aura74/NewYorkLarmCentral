Who We Are

Where Technology Meets Uncompromised Safety

Lars Larm Central was founded on a simple belief: no employee should ever feel unsafe at work. We've built the most advanced personal alarm platform in the industry — combining wearable alarm buttons, a powerful mobile app, and a state-of-the-art command center staffed by trained operators around the clock.

From lone workers in remote locations to hospital staff on night shifts, our ecosystem provides instant, GPS-pinpointed emergency response. One press of a button — or one tap in the app — and help is already on the way.
