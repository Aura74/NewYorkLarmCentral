Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 June 2026

Lars Résonance Atelier ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site or request a private audition, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, phone) when you request an audition or enquiry.
  • Your preferences stored locally in your browser — language, currency, theme, saved instruments and recently viewed pieces. These never leave your device.
  • Basic, anonymous usage information needed to operate the site.

How we use it

We use your contact details solely to respond to your enquiry and arrange your visit. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties except where required to deliver a service you have requested.

Local storage

Settings such as language and currency, your saved instruments and recently viewed pieces are kept in your browser's local storage for your convenience. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you by contacting atelier@larsresonance.com.
